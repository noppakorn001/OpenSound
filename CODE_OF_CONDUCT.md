# Community code of conduct

OpenSound welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss technical ideas with evidence, credit other people's work and give constructive feedback. Respect contributors' privacy and consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, spam and deliberate disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

The repository owner and appointed maintainers moderate project spaces. They may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, warn participants or restrict participation when needed. Decisions should be proportionate and explained without republishing private details. Good-faith disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome.

For an ordinary public conduct concern, ask a maintainer for moderation in the affected discussion without repeating offensive or private content. For a sensitive concern, use GitHub's report-abuse tools; the project does not currently operate a private conduct mailbox. Do not open a public issue containing personal information. Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
